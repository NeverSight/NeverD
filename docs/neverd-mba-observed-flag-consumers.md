# mba observed flag consumers

## Ownership and behavior

The shared MBA simplifier projects single observed flag windows, including
flattened AND and complemented OR consumers. Exact constant cofactors recover
one Boolean input through arithmetic tags. A bounded local completion visits
new consumers exposed by region measurement under the original work and
storage allowance, without repeating the whole MBA search. Every observed
window, and the finished reading itself, is kept only when it is not longer
than its input; a window that drops no lane would spell every input again as
an extract and hide the mask from the exact known-bits fold.

## Reproduction and validation

`SymMBAObservedWindow.FinishesFlagWindowsExposedByArithmeticMeasurement`
checks packed unrelated flags, flattened consumers and complemented masks.
`SymConditionalControls.ArithmeticTagsRecoverThePredicate` checks multiple
widths and independent-input counterexamples.
`SymMBAObservedWindow.UnreducedWindowsKeepTheirComplementedWord` checks that
such a window neither hides a complement from its consumer nor discards a
shortened word beneath it. `FinishedReadingsDoNotReplaceShorterWords` checks the
same for the finished comparison, and `LongerWindowsLeaveMasksToKnownBits` a
mask over a small sum, also behind a known-OR subtraction. Run the observed-window, conditional-control
and complete symbolic suites.
