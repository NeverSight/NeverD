The generated Windows kernel type libraries include declarations derived from
sdk/include/ddk/fltkernel.h in ReactOS, commit
b6224c3165811265422dacd5ed0de36b1c2311c9:
https://github.com/reactos/reactos/blob/b6224c3165811265422dacd5ed0de36b1c2311c9/sdk/include/ddk/fltkernel.h

WindowsKernelARM64.h also derives from the public-domain core DDK templates
under sdk/include/xdk at that revision, including wdm.template.h,
ntddk.template.h, ntifs.template.h and ntdef.template.h and their included
declarations. The source manifest is scripts/windows_types/reactos-arm64.sha256.
Core DDK contributors include Amine Khaldi and Timo Kreuzer.
The driver annotations in sdk/include/ddk/kernelspecs.h,
sdk/include/psdk/driverspecs.h and sdk/include/psdk/suppress.h are also public
domain; kernelspecs.h and suppress.h credit Timo Kreuzer.
The public-domain disclaimer below applies to these DDK headers too.
